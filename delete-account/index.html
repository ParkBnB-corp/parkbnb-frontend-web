Account Deletion & Data Erasure Policy

Delete Your ParkBnB Account & Associated Data

ParkBnB India is committed to user data privacy and Google Play developer compliance. You can permanently close your account and delete all personal records using the instructions below.

Login Retracted Immediately
Your login sessions, authentication tokens, and mobile app access are immediately retracted and deactivated upon receipt of your request.
⏳
Data Deleted in 90 Days
All personal identifiers, vehicle garage fleet data, and documents are permanently purged from primary servers within 90 days of request.
✉️
Reply in 24 Hours
Submit your closure request to support@parkbnb.space. Our support team will review and reply with confirmation within 24 hours.
